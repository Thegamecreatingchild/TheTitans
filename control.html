Control mode

Click the video area first so your keystrokes are captured, then hold W/A/S/D to translate and Q/E to rotate. K engages the dribbler if it's enabled on the robot. Releasing all keys stops the bot. Keys stop being sent if this tab loses focus.

Debug

Press V (anywhere, either mode) to print the current ball vector (centre→ball offset in px) to the Pi's console. Press M to toggle motor debug logging on the Pi. The live readout is now shown overlaid on the video feed (top-left) instead of down here.
